Your Weekly Rhythm

Mon
Metabolic Monday
6:00 PM PT with Coach IJ · All Members
▼
Weekly lesson + live Q&A. IJ teaches one topic from the 26-week rotation (nutrition, nervous system, movement, mindset, and more), then opens the floor. Bring your questions. This is the call where you learn why the program works, not just what to do.

Membership clients: This is your weekly call. Show up live or watch the recording in your Toolkit.
Wed
Win Wednesday
12:00 PM PT with Coach Soni / Wife · VIP/Bundle
▼
Your mid-week hype session. Share wins (especially the small ones), shout out someone in the group, get a live workout from Coach Soni, or just show up and soak in the energy. Soni hosts weeks 1 and 3, IJ's wife hosts weeks 2 and 4.

Format rotates: live workout, client spotlight, community shoutouts, movement demos.

Friday Reset
10:00 AM PT with Coach IJ · VIP/Bundle
▼
End your week with intention. Rotating format: client audits (IJ reviews your check-in live), hot girl walk (audio-only walking call), Q&A deep dive, or mindset session. This is the call where you get personal attention and set up your weekend for success.

Can't attend live? All calls are recorded and saved in your Toolkit within 24 hours.
