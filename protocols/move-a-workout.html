The workout you missed

A shift ate Tuesday. Move Tuesday. This is the whole thing.

  1. 1
    Tap the calendar tab at the bottom Bottom of your screen, next to the dashboard. That is your week.
  2. 2
    Scroll to the workout you missed and select it It is still sitting there on the day it was scheduled. Nothing has been deleted and nothing has expired.
  3. 3
    Top right, tap the three dots Same place on every workout screen, upper right corner.
  4. 4
    Tap Move To Another Day It is in the menu that slides up.
  5. 5

Where it turns up afterwards

Whenever you are in your dashboard you will see that workout there and be able to complete it, the same as any other day. Nothing about it is marked late and nothing counts against you. It is just your workout, on a different day.

The day you already know is going to be chaos

Exactly the same taps. The only difference is you are moving it BEFORE the day eats it.

  1. 1
    Select the workout on the day you know will not happen A double shift, a school thing, the day you fly. You already know on Sunday which day it is going to be.
  2. 2
    Three dots, Move To Another Day Same menu, same place.
  3. 3
    Move it earlier or later in the week Earlier is usually the better bet. A workout moved forward gets done; a workout moved back competes with whatever else lands on Friday.
  4. 4
    Tap Move, and you will see it on the day you picked Your week now matches the week you are actually going to have.

This is the skill, not the workaround

Moving sessions on purpose, around a real life, is what a shift-proof plan looks like. The people who keep going are not the ones who never miss. They are the ones who move it and carry on.

Where to put it

Same workout, wrong day, and you have set yourself up to miss it twice.

Nights and rotating

Not on a shift day if you can avoid it, and not on the day you flip. Your first day off after a stretch of nights is usually a write-off for training and that is fine. Aim for your second day off, or the morning before a shift rather than after one. If your only option is a shift day, move it there and use the five-minute too-tired version. Something small on a hard day beats a perfect session that never happens.

Days and everything else

Move it earlier in the week, not later. Later means it stacks up against everything else that got pushed, and Sunday ends up carrying three sessions and doing none. If the week is genuinely full, move it to the next week and take the four sessions you can do instead of chasing five you cannot.

What to do when it is not that simple

These come up every week. None of them are a reason to lose the session.

Do not move all three. Move one, do it, and let the other two go.

Three make-up sessions in one week is a punishment schedule, and it is the fastest way to quit. One session done is worth more to your body than three you resent.

Move it into next week and tell me.

A week where you did two instead of three is a normal week. I would rather know it happened than watch you disappear for a fortnight over it.

Do the five-minute too-tired version instead, and count it.

The point of the session is the signal you send your body, not the sweat. Five minutes tells it the same thing on a day when fifteen was never happening.

Move it tomorrow morning and carry on.

Nothing expires. A workout sitting on a past day is not a failure sitting in your app, it is just a workout waiting for a date.
