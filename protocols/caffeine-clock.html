Set the hour you want to be asleep

Everything below counts backwards from that. Not from your shift, and not from the clock on the wall, because your body only cares about the gap.

    Why eight hours and not four

    Caffeine has a half life of five to six hours, so half of your 2pm coffee is still working at 8pm and a quarter of it at bedtime. That leftover quarter does not keep you awake. It keeps you in lighter sleep all night, which is why you can sleep eight hours and still wake up flattened.

    The one number to hold

    400mg a day, and none of it inside the eight hours before sleep. If you only remember one line off this page, that is the line. Pregnancy, blood pressure medication, and heart rhythm issues all change that ceiling, so ask your own provider if any of those are you.

    When moving it is harder than it sounds

    Find yours. Do the one thing.

    Do not cut the amount. Move the timing first and hold the total.

    Withdrawal headaches come from cutting the dose, not from moving it earlier. Get the timing right for two weeks, then reduce the amount if you still want to. Doing both at once is how people quit on day three.

    Eat instead, and check what you had before the shift.

    Hour ten fatigue is almost always the meal you skipped before you clocked in. Caffeine there is a loan against your sleep, and the interest is tomorrow.

    Correct, and that is the point. Front-load it instead.

    On a night shift the cutoff genuinely does land mid-shift. Move your biggest cup to the first half rather than trying to spread it out, and the second half gets easier once your sleep improves.

    Try the cutoff for seven nights and compare how you feel at hour ten, not how fast you fall asleep.

    Caffeine rarely stops fast sleepers falling asleep. It flattens the deep sleep underneath, and you cannot feel that directly. You feel it as needing more caffeine tomorrow.

    Cut one, not four, and cut the latest one first.

    The last one of the day costs you the most and is the easiest to lose. Take that one out for a week before touching any of the others.

    Anchor on sleep, never on the shift. Recalculate the one number every time your sleep target moves.

    It is the only rule that survives a rotating schedule, and it is why this page asks for your sleep time rather than your clock-in.
