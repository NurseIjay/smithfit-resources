You are not weighing anything

A palm of meat is roughly 25 to 30g. A scoop of powder is 25g. A cup of Greek yogurt is 20g. Count palms and scoops, not grams. Four of those in a day and you are there.

The whole review, honestly

Any third-party-tested powder you will actually drink beats the perfect one you will not. That is genuinely the entire answer, and I am not going to pretend otherwise to sound more thorough.

Third-party tested means somebody outside the company checked that what is on the label is in the tub. Look for NSF Certified for Sport or Informed Choice on the packaging. That is the only box worth ticking.

Whey is the default and it works. If dairy wrecks your stomach, whey isolate is usually fine because most of the lactose is gone, and if it still is not, a plant blend is genuinely no worse. Nobody has ever failed at this because they picked the wrong powder.
