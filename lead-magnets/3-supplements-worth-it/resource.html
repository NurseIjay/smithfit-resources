The keeper card

Protein, creatine, vitamin D. Everything else on the shelf is optional at best. Doses, prices per day, and what the label has to prove before it earns your card.

The SmithFit Keeper Card Fridge door. Locker. Done.
🥤 Protein powder 1 scoop = about 25g protein

It is food in a faster container, not a magic shake. Whey or a plant blend both work. Its job is to plug the gap your shift creates, not to replace meals you would otherwise eat.

The math: 0.7 to 1.0g of protein per pound of goal weight, so a 160 lb goal means 112 to 160g a day. Over 45, live near the top. Type your goal weight in Part 2 and I will run your exact number.

Buy20g or more protein per scoop, protein first on the ingredient list, third-party tested, label short enough to read on a break.

💪 Creatine 3 to 5g daily, any time, about $0.10 a day

Timing is irrelevant, the daily streak is everything. Take it on nights, days, and days off, and never bother loading.

Why a nurse specifically: your brain runs on the same energy stores creatine tops off, and small trials show it blunts the mental fog of short sleep. Short sleep is the job.

BuyPlain creatine monohydrate, one ingredient, NSF or Informed Sport seal. Skip gummy forms: the dose rarely survives the candy-making. The cheapest sealed tub is the right tub.

☀️ Vitamin D 1000 to 2000 IU, with a fat-containing meal

You clock in before sunrise and drive home after dark, and shift workers barely see sun. It absorbs with fat, so it rides with eggs, peanut butter, or your pre-shift plate. Not with black coffee.

The nurse move: you draw labs for a living. Get a 25-OH-D drawn before going above 2000 IU, then dose to your number instead of the marketing.

BuyThe D3 form. The $8 bottle is the same molecule as the $30 one.

Educational resource, not medical advice. Run these past your provider, especially with prescriptions, kidney concerns, or pregnancy.

Part 6

When the plan breaks

Because it will, and that is fine. The plan for the four most common breakdowns:

You missed creatine three days in a row

Resume at 3 to 5g and move on. Your stores drain over weeks, not days, and there is no catching up to do. Never double the dose.

Money is tight this month

Keep vitamin D and creatine, $22 a month combined, and let the protein powder lapse. It is the one keeper food replaces: 2 eggs (12g) plus a Greek yogurt cup (15g) covers a scoop.

Three nights deep and wrecked

That is not a supplement problem, so do not buy anything. Protein at the 3am break, water, blackout curtains at 8:30am. Melatonin only if this is your flip day, 0.5 to 1mg.

You bought a gummy version again

No shame, they are engineered to be bought. Finish it as candy, count it as candy, and rebuy the capsule or powder with a seal on the label.
