Part 1

The keeper card

Protein, creatine, vitamin D. Everything else on the shelf is optional at best. Here is exactly how to run the three, in plain language.

The SmithFit Keeper Card Fridge door. Locker. Done.
🥤 Protein powder 1 scoop = 25 to 30g protein

It is convenient food, not a magic shake. Aim it at your daily protein target, and lean on it hardest the shifts where lunch never happens. Whey, casein, or a plant blend all work.

Buy20g or more protein per scoop, protein first on the ingredient list, third-party tested.

💪 Creatine 3 to 5g every day

Timing does not matter, the daily streak does. Take it on nights, days, and days off. The most studied supplement there is: muscle and strength, plus early signals for a sleep-deprived brain.

BuyPlain creatine monohydrate. One ingredient on the label. The cheapest tub is the right tub.

☀️ Vitamin D 1000 to 2000 IU, with food

You clock in before sunrise and drive home after dark, and shift workers barely see sun. Take it with a meal that has some fat. You know the move: get a level drawn before going higher.

BuyD3 form. The $8 bottle is the same molecule as the $30 one.

Educational resource, not medical advice. Run these past your provider, especially with prescriptions, kidney concerns, or pregnancy.

What to cancel first

In order. Start at the top, cancel one tonight, and do not replace it with anything.

1
Fat burner$45/mo back

Detox tea$30/mo back

Your liver was never waiting on a tea. This one is a laxative with branding.

3

4

5
BCAAs$30/mo back

Already inside the protein you eat. Fully redundant.

6
Collagen marketed for weight loss$35/mo back

Fine as coffee flavor. Useless for fat loss, weak as a protein.
