When to actually adjust

Most people change the plan two weeks too early, then call the plan broken. These are the rules I hold my clients to.

  1. Never adjust off one week. One week is a single data point. It does not get a vote on your plan.
  2. Trending down at all? Change nothing. Even 0.3 lbs a week is the right direction. Slow down is still down.
  3. Flat means two straight weeks. Weekly averages within about half a pound of each other, twice in a row. That is the earliest moment an adjustment is earned.
  4. Change one thing, then wait two weeks. A little more protein, a shorter grazing window after shift, one more walk. One change, two weeks of data, then judge it.
  5. Chaos weeks get an asterisk, not an adjustment. Sick kid, travel, period week, a stretch of nights. Read around them, the way you read around a vital taken mid-transfer.

The chart says when. The call is what and how.
