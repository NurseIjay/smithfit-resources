The 3am order is exhaustion, not weakness

By 3am you have been awake most of a day, the floor finally goes quiet, and your body is at its lowest point of the night, screaming for fast fuel. Of course pizza sounds like survival. Every night shifter knows that call.

Here is the part nobody told you: the same meal hits your body harder at 3am than it does at 6pm. Big food in the deep night is one of the habits most tied to weight gain in shift workers. The fix is not more willpower at 3am. It is a bigger meal at 5pm.

This is a window, not a fasting plan. Fasting through a 12 is how you end up shaky at 4am pushing meds. You eat all night. You eat on purpose.
