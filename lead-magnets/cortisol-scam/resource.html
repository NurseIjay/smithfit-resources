The $0 Reset SmithFit · IJ Smith, RN
Days
  • One wake time, every day, within an hour
  • Ten minutes of outdoor light before 9am, dim the house after 8pm
  • Protein breakfast, real lunch, nothing heavy 2 to 3 hours before bed
  • Train before shifts or on off days. After a 12, walk.
Nights
  • Protect a 4-hour anchor sleep window (9am to 1pm) every day
  • Sunglasses home, blackout room, bright light at shift start
  • Main meal pre-shift, keep 2am to 5am light, small snack before day-sleep
  • Sleep first, then train. Short sessions on night stretches.
Rotating
  • Anchor within each block, reset the anchor when the block changes
  • Light when your day starts, dark when it ends, yours not the sun's
  • Meals on body time: waking, personal midday, personal evening
  • Train mid-block. Flip days are for sleep, food, and walks.
Before you buy calm, ask 1. Diagnosed by a quiz, not a lab?   2. Proof is a story, not a study?   3. Renews monthly?   4. Promises sleep results without sleep?   5. Would it be medicine if it worked?
The rule that survives any week Keep the sleep anchor, drop the rest guilt-free, rebuild next week.
