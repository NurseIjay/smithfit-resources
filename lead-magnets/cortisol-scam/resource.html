02 / What to expect

The honest timeline

No jar can print a timeline like this, because a jar cannot do any of it. The fix lands in a predictable order, and knowing the order is what keeps you from quitting in week two:

Week 1
Falling asleep gets easier first. The anchor and the light do that part before anything else moves. The scale may dip too; early drops are mostly water, so do not hang anything on them.
Week 2
Energy steadies through the back half of shifts. Hours 8 through 12 stop feeling like a cliff you fall off.
Weeks 3 to 4
The cravings quiet down. Not because you got tougher, but because the sleep and the fuel stopped whipsawing each other.

04 / The one-pager

Print it, stick it in your locker

The whole system on one page: every pattern with its clock times, the checklist, and the rule that survives any week.

The $0 Reset SmithFit · IJ Smith, RN
Days
  • Sleep 10:15pm to 5:45am, every night, days off too
  • Outside light by 6:15am (10 minutes is enough), dim the house from 8:15pm
  • 27g protein by 6:15am, real lunch at 12pm (35g), landing plate 8:15pm, caffeine off by 2:15pm
  • Lift only on 6+ hours of sleep, otherwise a 20 minute walk. Real lifts live on off days.
Nights
  • Sleep 9am to 4:30pm daily, plus the blackout drill: curtains or mask, do not disturb, cold room, no scroll
  • Sunglasses 7:30 to 8am. Bright light at 3am is fine; sun after clock-out is the enemy
  • Real dinner 5pm (35g), keep midnight to 3am light, landing bite 8:30am, caffeine off by 1am
  • Never lift after the 3rd night in a row. Lift the first off day, after the window, around 5:30pm.
Rotating
  • One core window: 1:45 to 5:45, am on day blocks, pm on night blocks. One 12-hour flip, no drift
  • Flip days: outdoor light at your NEW morning, whatever the wall clock says
  • Biggest meal pre-shift, midnight to 3am light, caffeine off 8 hours before whichever bed is next
  • Lift mid-block only. Flip days are walking days. Never after the 3rd night.
The times above assume 7 o'clock starts with a 30 minute drive. Different start? Slide every time with it. The tool above writes your exact version.
