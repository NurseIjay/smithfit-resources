Strawberry Dessert Trio

Three real desserts built around fresh strawberries, made for the days you want something sweet and don't want to undo your progress to get it.
Strawberry Season Desserts
Each option lands 260-310 cal with 20g+ protein
Pick one after dinner, after a shift, or pack one to go when the craving hits.
Strawberry Shortcake Yogurt Parfait280 cal · 24P / 30C / 6F
Ingredients
Serves 1
  • 1 cup (150g) fresh strawberries, sliced
  • 3/4 cup (170g) plain nonfat Greek yogurt
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 1/4 cup (30g) crumbled angel food cake or vanilla wafers
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
How to make it
5 minutes, no cook
  • Stir vanilla extract into Greek yogurt until smooth
  • Layer yogurt, sliced strawberries, and cake crumbles in a glass, repeat once more
  • Drizzle honey over the top and serve right away
Strawberry Cheesecake Protein Mousse310 cal · 28P / 18C / 12F
Ingredients
Serves 1
  • 1/2 cup (115g) light whipped cream cheese
  • 1 scoop (30g) vanilla protein powder
  • 1/2 cup (85g) strawberries, diced small
  • 2 tbsp (30ml) unsweetened almond milk
  • 1 tbsp (7g) graham cracker crumbs, for topping
How to make it
8 minutes, no cook
  • Whip cream cheese, protein powder, and almond milk together until fluffy
  • Fold in half the diced strawberries
  • Spoon into a small dish, top with remaining strawberries and graham crumbs, chill 10 minutes if you have it
Chocolate-Dipped Strawberry Protein Bites260 cal · 20P / 24C / 10F
Ingredients
Makes 6 bites
  • 12 medium strawberries, hulled and halved
  • 1/3 cup (60g) dark chocolate chips (60% cacao)
  • 1 scoop (30g) vanilla protein powder
  • 1 tbsp (15g) coconut oil
  • Pinch of sea salt
How to make it
15 minutes, chill 20
  • Melt chocolate and coconut oil together in short microwave bursts, stirring between
  • Whisk in protein powder and sea salt until smooth and glossy
  • Dip each strawberry half in the mix, set on parchment, chill in the fridge until firm

From Coach Team

Pick one of these and let it be the whole dessert, not a starting point. If you're logging, measure the Greek yogurt and cream cheese with a scale once so you know what a real portion looks like in your bowl, then you can eyeball it after that. The chocolate bites hold in the fridge for about 4 days, so make the batch of 6 and portion them out ahead of a busy stretch.
