Happy Nurses Week

May 6 - 12, 2026

This card isn't about nutrition or workouts. It's about you. The person behind the scrubs who shows up when it matters most.

A Letter from IJ

I want to say something that nobody at the hospital is going to say to you this week, at least not in a way that really lands.

You chose one of the hardest careers on the planet. Not because of the science or the hours (though those are brutal). Because you chose to carry other people's worst days on your shoulders, over and over, and still show up the next shift.

And somewhere along the way, you stopped taking care of yourself the way you take care of everyone else. That's not weakness. That's what happens when your entire identity is built around giving.

SmithFit exists because I watched it happen to me first. I watched my body change, my energy disappear, my confidence shrink. And I knew it wasn't laziness. It was the job. It was the stress. It was the system.

So this week, I don't want you to hit a PR or nail your macros. I want you to do one thing: recognize that you deserve the same care you give your patients.

That's it. That's the whole card.

Your Nurses Week Challenge

One act of self-care per day this week. Not fitness. Not nutrition. Just something that's for YOU.

  • MMonday: 10 minutes of silence before your shift. No phone.
  • TTuesday: Buy yourself something. Coffee, flowers, a book. Under $10.
  • WWednesday: Text a nurse friend and tell them one thing you admire about them.
  • TThursday: Say no to one thing that drains you. Just one.
  • FFriday: Look in the mirror and say "I'm proud of you" out loud. (It'll feel weird. Do it anyway.)
  • SSaturday: Do absolutely nothing productive for one hour. No guilt.
  • SSunday: Write down three things your body did for you this week. Thank it.
